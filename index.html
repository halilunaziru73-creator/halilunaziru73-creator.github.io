Digital Agriculture, Around the Field

problems farmers keep running into ▼

CRITIQUE

How Agricultural Intelligence Can Revolutionize Farming

World Economic Forum, Jan 2026

Points to a growing digital divide, large operations adopting AI quickly while many smallholders remain priced out.

CRITIQUE

Technology Diffusion in Agriculture

World Intellectual Property Report 2026, WIPO

Farmers face costs starting above EUR 16,500 just to implement one mapping technology, and risk being locked into a single vendor without interoperability standards. Some have abandoned precision tools entirely once the numbers stopped adding up.

CRITIQUE

Precision Agriculture: Benefits and Challenges for Technology Adoption and Use

U.S. Government Accountability Office

High upfront acquisition costs remain prohibitive for farmers with limited capital, and unresolved questions over who actually owns farm data continue to slow adoption of AI tools.

CRITIQUE

Agricultural Technology Adoption, New Tech for 2026

Farmonaut

Training, financing, and basic infrastructure such as rural connectivity remain unresolved barriers, especially for smallholder farmers who need equitable access, not just better tools.

CRITIQUE

Employee Perception of Precision Technology Use at the Dairy Farm

Peer reviewed study

A shortage of skilled labour able to operate precision tools, and language barriers for migrant farm workers, are consistently underestimated obstacles compared to the technology itself.
