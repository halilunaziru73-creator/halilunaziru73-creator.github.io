Naziru HALILU

Digital Agriculture

Agricultural engineer with a long-term research focus on the digitalization of agriculture, spanning computer vision, geospatial machine learning, agricultural control systems, and high-throughput phenotyping, grounded in a strong foundation in agricultural machinery, mechanization, and farm power systems. Current interests include self-supervised deep learning for crop-stress detection and vegetation-index estimation from UAV and multispectral imagery, and closed-loop sensing and control for irrigation, greenhouse automation, and precision farm machinery. This is complemented by field-based phenotyping pipelines, hybrid deep-learning architectures for environmental process modelling, and reproducible, uncertainty-aware ML pipelines built on GIS and satellite remote sensing.
